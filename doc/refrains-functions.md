# refrains: functions and documentation

The reference version of this doc is in French, see: [refrains-fonctions.fr.md](refrains-fonctions.fr.md)

The 53 functions exported by the `:refrains` package (version 0.2.1), grouped by the sections of the menu, with their arguments and their docstring (as printed by `doc` and `help`). The name of each function links to its description in the [documentation](refrains.md) when there is one.

The resemblances between these functions, by their names and by their documentation, are drawn in the [Function trees](#function-trees).

About terminology: for the sake of simplicity and consistency, the names of the functions may change with the versions and, as far as possible, refer to an etymology common to French and English, or even to Indo-European. Since version 0.2.0, the names follow two conventions: `family-object` (`filt-median`, `graph-path`, `dist-edit`, `motif-list`…) and `source>target` for conversions (`num>base`, `str>sym`, `graph>dot`, `dendrogram>dot`…). The sign `>` marks a change of representation, without analysis: the same content in another form (a number in base n, a list as a symbol, a graph as a matrix or a dot file). The hyphen marks an operation of a family, which computes something new: filtering, measuring a distance, looking for motifs, classifying (`graph-span`, `graph-part`, `graph-dendrogram`). The sign `>` reads "to" as well as « vers »: it does not depend on a language. For the functions on trees, *graph* was preferred to *tree*: the word is common to French and English (*graphe*, *graph*), graph theory applies to trees, and it is also used in developing refrains.

## Function trees

The 53 functions linked by their resemblances, with refrains itself: two
minimum spanning trees (`graph-span`), drawn with `graph>dot` and neato. The
shorter an edge, the closer the two functions. The dendrogram of each tree
(`graph-dendrogram`, single linkage) orders its fusions by increasing
distance: the leaves are on the left, and each fusion is placed at the
abscissa of its distance (`dendrogram>dot`). The file
[refrains-trees.lisp](lisp/refrains-trees.lisp) computes the trees again
and redraws the images: `(load "doc/lisp/refrains-trees.lisp")`.

**By their names**: normalized edit distance between the names, letter by
letter (`dist-edit` with `:norm t`). The families of names (`filt-`,
`graph-`, `dist-`, `mark-`, `motif-`) make branches.

![Minimum spanning tree of the refrains functions by their names](pictures/refrains-names.png)

![Dendrogram of the refrains functions by their names](pictures/refrains-names-dendrogram.png)

**By their documentation**: Jaccard distance between the vocabularies of the
docstrings, split into words with `separate`. The grammatical words of
English (articles and determiners, pronouns, auxiliaries, conjunctions,
prepositions: *the*, *it*, *is*, *and*, *of*…), the numbers and the
one-letter symbols (*a*, *b*, *x*…) are removed.
The variants of a same word are merged into a generic term, the shortest of
them (*list* and *lists*; *define*, *defined* and *defines*…): the words
whose relative edit distance (`dist-edit`, a substitution counting as a
deletion and an insertion) is at most 0.2, grouped with `graph-dendrogram`.
The words of the style of the docstrings, which come back in all the
functions that have options without saying what they do (*argument*,
*keyword*, *optional*, *default*, *nil*, *returns*, *define*…), are then
removed. All the other words count, even very frequent ones (*list*,
*distance*, *element*…). The groups there come from the vocabulary, no longer from the
names.

![Minimum spanning tree of the refrains functions by their documentation](pictures/refrains-docstrings.png)

In the dendrogram, the functions group together mostly by families (graphs,
distances, information, filters, marks and motifs, conversions), between
0.4 and 0.9: their docstrings share few words.

![Dendrogram of the refrains functions by their documentation](pictures/refrains-docstrings-dendrogram.png)
